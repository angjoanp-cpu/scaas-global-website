SCaaS Global Website

Official website repository for SCaaS Global.

SCaaS Global helps growing product-based and e-commerce businesses improve profitability, strengthen supply chain operations, manage inventory more effectively, and unlock cash flow through fractional supply chain leadership.

About SCaaS Global

SCaaS Global provides practical supply chain and operational support for businesses that need stronger systems without immediately building a large internal operations team.

Our focus includes:

Demand planning
Inventory management
Purchasing and procurement
Supplier management
Production planning
Logistics and distribution
Landed cost management
Cash flow optimization
Supply chain systems and process improvement
Fractional supply chain leadership

SCaaS Global Solutions

The website will also introduce supply chain tools and systems being developed by SCaaS Global, including:

StockVelocity

Inventory and demand planning platform designed to help businesses:

Forecast demand
Monitor inventory health

Manage production requirements
Reduce stockouts
Control excess inventory
Protect cash flow
VendorVelocity

Supplier and vendor management solution focused on:

Purchase order visibility
Production status
Vendor acknowledgment
Supplier performance
Issues and corrective actions
Vendor KPIs
TariffVelocity

Tariff classification and landed-cost planning solution designed to help businesses:

Research possible tariff classifications
Estimate duties and tariffs
Compare sourcing countries
Calculate estimated landed cost
Improve sourcing decisions
